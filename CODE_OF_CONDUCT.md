# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participation must remain respectful, technically honest, and safe for people of every
background and level of experience. Constructive participation includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- accepting correction and explaining disagreements with concrete technical reasoning;
- respecting privacy, licensing, restricted documentation, and security-reporting boundaries;
- distinguishing verified behavior from assumptions and incomplete hardware qualification; and
- helping make reviews and documentation understandable to contributors outside the original team.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
repeated disruption, or publishing another person's confidential information is unacceptable.
Using credentials, card data, restricted documents, or security findings to pressure or endanger
another participant is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, reviews, patches, project discussions, release channels,
and project-related public or private communication. It also applies when someone represents the
project at an event or in another community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using a private contact mechanism
published by the repository host or the maintainer's public profile. Do not put sensitive personal
details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports in confidence to the extent reasonably possible, gather relevant
context, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a clarification, warning,
content removal, temporary participation restriction, or permanent exclusion. A maintainer with a
conflict of interest must not decide the report. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in
an investigation is prohibited.

This policy creates community participation expectations; it does not replace the repository
license, hosting-platform terms, or applicable law.
